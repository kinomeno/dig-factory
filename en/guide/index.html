Dig Factory Tycoon guide: a free vertical mining & factory game

Dig Factory Tycoon (ほりほり工場) is a browser game about digging down with drills, carrying resources up with conveyors, processing them in machines and selling them by truck. It runs on phones and PCs with no install, and it is free.

You start as a lone boss with a shovel. Place drills, hire workers, research new machines and break through harder and harder bedrock.

What you do

  • Dig: drills dig soil, stone, iron, gold, diamonds and more. Deeper layers hide rare finds and fossils.
  • Carry: conveyors, sorters, elevators, forklifts and trucks. Building the flow is a relaxing puzzle.
  • Process: 18 machines turn raw resources into 50+ products, from bricks and iron bars to gems, RC cars and even houses.
  • Hire: workers walk to drills and machines, get better as they work, and rest at the office when tired.
  • Research: unlock machines and perks at the lab, raise your company level and break bedrock.
  • Idle friendly: the factory keeps running while you are away (30 minutes at first, up to a day with research).
  • Languages: English, Japanese (kanji) and a simple Japanese hiragana mode for kids.

Getting started

  1. Open the game and press "New game".
  2. The boss digs the first hole for you. Follow the guide and place a drill.
  3. Carry resources up with conveyors and sell them by truck. Then grow your company your own way.

How to play (same as the in-game help)

Controls

Swipe the screen to scroll (or press the screen edges). Pinch, Ctrl+wheel or the magnifier buttons to zoom; "Map" shows everything.
Pick tools from the tabs at the bottom. Drills and machines are deselected after placing one; conveyors stay selected until you press the "x" (bottom left) or a tab. For a drill, tap the spot, pick a direction, then Place.

Digging

Tap a spot, pick a direction and Place to put a drill. Mined items come out behind the drill and fall through the air.
Bedrock 0 is at 5m (break it at company level 1), then every 10m (research Dig level). Until then drills keep running and give resources at half speed. Above 5m there is only soil. Tap a drill to stop or start it.

Moving

Conveyors carry items; a sorter sends the chosen item to its side.
Items that reach the surface are dropped there; forklifts carry them to the yard, and you get paid when the truck returns.

Processing

Machines take input from any side but the front and output to the front. Items they do not accept pass through at half speed.
You start with the Brick kiln and the Crusher; research more at the Lab.

Workers

Add Lv0 workers at the office. They walk to drills and machines and level up as they work; places without one show "z" (stopped) or "A" (running slowly on their own).
From Lv2 they also take surface jobs (forklifts, trucks, the Lab). Tired workers rest at the office. Wages are monthly; if you cannot pay, one quits.

Office & Lab

Tap the office to upgrade it: more staff, more forklifts, faster rest. At level 2 you can build the Lab (1 cell, anywhere on the surface) from the Facilities tab.
Research machines, batch upgrades and skills at the Lab; without a Lv2+ researcher it is very slow. Buy forklifts from "Upgrade".

Money

You get paid when the truck returns. If money is low and sales are zero, the bank lends you some (repaid from sales).
"Info" shows graphs. Save from Settings. Tap the title to reset.

Oil & hot springs

Below bedrock 3, digging out a cell may reveal a hot spring (oil below bedrock 4). Nothing can be built on a source. Connect it with pipes up to the first underground row to fill the surface tank.
On the surface you can build a Hot spring, a Power plant and a Refinery. Surface facilities are very slow without a worker. Need room? Research Land purchase.
