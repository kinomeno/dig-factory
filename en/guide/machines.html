Surface facilities

  • Hot spring：Uses hot water. Guests come by car and pay when they leave.
  • Power plant：Burns hot water (geothermal) or oil and sells the power on the spot.
  • Refinery：2 oil make 1 fuel. Fuel goes to the yard and sells by truck.
  • Museum：Shows the rare things you dig up. Visitors pay admission (more kinds, higher fee). Duplicates sell at the shop ×1.5.
  • Home center：Shelves products from the yard (up to 10 of each). Customers drive in and pay 1.5x the shipping price.
