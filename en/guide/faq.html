Dig Factory Tycoon FAQ

Is Dig Factory Tycoon free?

Yes. It is free to play and needs no install. Just open the page in a browser such as Safari, Chrome or Edge.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. It works on iPhone, Android and PC. Add it to your home screen to play full screen like an app.

How does saving work?

Your progress is saved automatically in your browser. To move to a new device, use Export in Settings and Import the file on the new device.

Does it progress while I am away?

Yes. The factory keeps running while you are away: 30 minutes at first, up to a full day with the Longer idle research.

Is it OK for kids?

Yes. There is a simple Japanese hiragana mode, and the boss walks new players through the controls.

Which languages are supported?

English, Japanese (kanji) and Japanese hiragana. Choose on the title screen.

What kind of game is it?

A vertical mining game combined with factory building and management simulation, with relaxing idle-game growth.

Who made it?

Kinomeno. More games and tools are at kinomeno.com.
